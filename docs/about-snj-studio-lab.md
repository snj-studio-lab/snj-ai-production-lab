# About S&J Studio Lab

S&J Studio Lab is a public lab for AI-assisted cinematic video production.

It is run by a solo creator building longform and shortform AI video workflows through real channel operations.

The project documents the parts of AI video production that are usually hidden:

- failed prompts
- broken scene structures
- reference-image mistakes
- character consistency problems
- subtitle cleanup
- production gates
- workflow revisions
- lessons from shipping real videos

The goal is not to present AI video as easy or automatic.

The goal is to show how the work actually evolves.

## Creator position

I work at the intersection of three roles:

- AI video creator
- solo studio workflow designer
- public production lab operator

Before building AI video workflows, I spent nearly two decades working in practical business operations, dealing with process, cost, documentation, people, risk, and execution.

That operational background shapes how I approach AI production.

For me, AI video is not only a creative problem.

It is a production system problem.

## What I document

S&J Studio Lab focuses on:

- how AI video workflows break
- how production rules are rewritten
- how prompts become safer and more executable
- how cinematic scenes fail or survive in real tools
- how a solo creator can build repeatable systems

This is a working record, not a finished course.

The process is part of the asset.
